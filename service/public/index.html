Campaign Tools

Matt Grant for Congress — MO-02 primary, August 4, 2026

A Chrome side-panel for voter registration & get-out-the-vote work.

Get the extension

⬇ Download for Chrome

Works in Chrome, Edge, and Brave on a computer (not phones).

Install it (about 2 minutes)

  1. Unzip the file you just downloaded. You'll get a folder named matt-grant-campaign-tools. Keep it somewhere you won't delete (deleting the folder removes the extension).
  2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions (type it into the address bar).
  3. Turn on Developer mode — the switch in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked (top-left) and choose the matt-grant-campaign-tools folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin "Campaign Tools" so its icon stays visible.
💡 That "Developer mode" step is normal for a campaign tool that isn't in the public Chrome store. It's safe.

Start using it

  1. Click the Campaign Tools icon in your toolbar — the panel opens on the right side of your browser.
  2. When the panel opens, sign in with your campaign email. (There's nothing to configure — it already knows where to connect.)
  3. The first time you sign in, an admin gives you a role. Until then you'll see a limited view. Message your campaign admin to get switched on.
  4. Once your role is set, your tabs appear — do your work, and you're set.

What each role can do

RegistrationReach voters about registering; send approved registration texts.
Voter contactGOTV outreach and logging voter conversations.
List dataImport and tag voter lists.
EventsManage events and volunteer shifts.
CommsDraft and send approved messages.
ComplianceApprovals, opt-outs, and the audit log.
AdminEverything, including assigning roles.
🛡️ Built-in guardrails: texts only go out during allowed hours, messages need approval, and anyone who opts out is removed automatically.

Something not working?

  1. "Signed in but nothing shows" — your role isn't set yet. Ask an admin.
  2. The extension disappeared — the folder was moved or deleted. Unzip again and re-do "Load unpacked."
  3. Still stuck — contact your campaign admin.
