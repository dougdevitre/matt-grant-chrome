Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

This policy covers the Matt Grant for Congress — Campaign Tools Chrome extension and its backend service. The tool is an internal productivity aid for authorized campaign clerks and volunteers for Missouri's 2nd Congressional District. It is not a consumer product and is not directed to the general public or to children.

What the extension stores

  • Your own settings — the backend Service URL, your selected MO-02 location (county, school district, ZIP), and UI preferences such as the "site tips" toggle. These are kept in Chrome storage on your device so the panel remembers them.
  • Your sign-in session — the extension uses cookies on the campaign's authentication provider (Clerk) so you stay signed in. This is required by the Clerk sign-in library.
  • The active tab's site name (hostname only) — when "site tips" is on, the panel checks whether the tab you're viewing is a campaign-related site (for example the Missouri Secretary of State site, a county election office, or a tool like Airtable) so it can show a relevant tip. It reads only the hostname — never the page address path, the page content, or your history — and only for the specific sites the extension is granted access to (listed as host_permissions in the extension's manifest). Turn this off anytime with the "site tips" toggle in Settings.

The extension does not collect your browsing history, read the content of pages you visit, or run any analytics or tracking. It ships no secrets and no remotely hosted code.

Data handled by the backend

All privileged work happens on a secured backend, not in the browser. When clerks use the tool for campaign tasks, the backend may process and store campaign records (for example, contact lists a clerk imports, task and event data, and message templates). This data:

  • is restricted to campaign and election purposes, consistent with Missouri law governing voter-list use;
  • is accessible only to authorized clerks according to their role;
  • honors opt-out requests — recipients who opt out are recorded and excluded from further outreach; and
  • for any outbound messaging, carries the required "Paid for by" disclaimer and follows applicable TCPA consent and opt-out rules.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or campaign data to third parties.
  • We do not use or transfer data for purposes unrelated to the tool's single purpose.
  • We do not use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We do not load remote code; the extension runs only its bundled, reviewed scripts.

Third-party services

The tool integrates with services the campaign operates or relies on — for example Clerk (sign-in) and Airtable (campaign-record storage), and, when enabled, Google Calendar/Gmail and an SMS provider for outreach. Public-data lookups (such as the U.S. Census geocoder) and official voter resources are linked out to — for example the Missouri Secretary of State site for registration and polling places — and are never scraped or re-hosted. Each service's own privacy terms apply to data it processes.

Access, retention, and questions

Access is limited to authorized campaign staff. To ask about the data the tool holds about you, to request correction or deletion, or with any privacy question, contact the campaign at privacy@mattgrantforcongress.org or reach your campaign admin. Records are retained only as long as needed for campaign operations and any applicable legal requirements.
